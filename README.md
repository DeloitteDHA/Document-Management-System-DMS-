# 📄 Document Management System (DMS)

This **Document Management System (DMS)** is built to support immigration officers and adjudicators within the **Department of Home Affairs** as they process visa applications.

## 📘 Purpose

The DMS provides all necessary documentation and guidance to ensure that adjudicators:

- Understand and follow **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)**
- Operate in full compliance with **South African immigration laws and policies**

## 🛠️ Key Features

- Seamless navigation of the **Visa Adjudication System (VAS)**
- Clear steps to **review and verify applicant information**
- Guidance to make **informed decisions** on visa approvals or rejections.
- Assurance of **compliance with regulations** and internal protocols.

## ✅ Outcome

By following this manual, adjudicators will be equipped to carry out their responsibilities efficiently and accurately, ensuring integrity and consistency throughout the visa application process.
